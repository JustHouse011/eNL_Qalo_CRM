# Glass surface review — 30 September 2026

Light-mode refinement: content is now 58% white, navigation 72%, and overlays 91%, with 130% saturation and 20–24px desktop blur. Broader static mint/purple gradients remain visible through the surfaces. Nested light cards use a 28% white wash with no extra blur, while reading surfaces stay opaque. Dark-theme tokens are unchanged. The refreshed 48-route/theme audit reported no page errors or horizontal overflow; a targeted light-mode contrast rerun measured a minimum of 5.71:1 across the sampled surface/text pairs (see `research/glass/contrast-audit.json`). Build passed. This styling-only refinement did not rerun the interaction suite described below.

`src/glass.css` is the shared surface layer. Layout and state logic are unchanged; report chart colours now consume theme-aware tokens. The local-service regression test now checks its configured origin rather than a hardcoded port, permitting isolated test servers without falsely treating local requests as external services.

The browser audit visited 24 routes in light and dark token scopes, including the public preview as a regression check. Standalone support screens normally inherit dark tokens; their light scope was applied by the audit to check compatibility without adding a theme control. No page errors or page-level horizontal overflow were observed. Inbox widths of 390 and 768px were also checked. Screenshots include dialogs, profile menus and calendar overlays. See `research/glass/` and `research/glass/audit.json`.

Contrast was measured using pixels sampled from rendered navigation/content/overlay probes over the actual workspace, compared with the computed primary, muted, secondary-muted and accent text tokens. All 24 sampled pairs exceed 4.5:1. The minimum was 5.88:1 in light mode and 7.51:1 in dark mode. These are sampled surface checks, not a claim of exhaustive accessibility certification. Tables, fields and message bubbles use opaque surfaces to avoid background-dependent reading contrast.

The audit helpers are `research/audit-glass.mjs` and `research/review-glass.mjs`. They use the running development app on localhost:5174 and save artifacts locally. The review helper exercises the solid fallback branch and Chromium's reduced-transparency/reduced-motion emulation. Native browsers without backdrop-filter still require cross-browser verification; their solid fallback styles are provided.

Final chart screenshots are `research/glass/light-charts.png` and `dark-charts.png`. Line and pie entrance animations are disabled so charts render immediately and do not rely on JavaScript motion that bypasses reduced-motion CSS. Disabled controls use opaque surfaces and readable text rather than fading the entire control.

Build passed. Lint completed with the existing React warnings. All 13 selected interaction regressions passed across the main run and the corrected origin-check rerun. Coverage includes customer/agent replies, private notes, ticket creation, contact edits, article persistence, CSV downloads, routing, business filters, profile access and mobile navigation. The public website route and frontend mock service were not changed by the surface system.
